# Shared-terminal Borel all-cut bridge

Global split: if any selected chord has |h|>=1/5, its anchored triangle has area |h|/2>=1/10. Otherwise all directions are hard, |h|<1/5.

For the finite hard block 99/100<=M<8/5, terminal receipts use one class-independent radial price and one application of the H-TC all-cut theorem with the global-worst kernel. There is no class-relative t coordinate and no exclusive first-birth owner. The low receipt is one radial column, not a tiled two-sheet increment. Remaining finite/full-span and exact-pole columns, if nonzero in the frozen witness, are pure radial columns with class-global kernels fixed before cuts. The tail uses half-open dyadic radial bands and the inherited all-scale tail theorem.

For each live radial column c and every Borel cut A, its source-resolved theorem has the form
  integral_A integral p_c(r) kappa_c(q,dr)dq <= integral_{W(A)} p_c(|x|)dnu(x).
The frozen certifier verifies sum_c p_c(r)<=1 on every common radial atom and verifies the total demand receipt >=gamma for all continuous parameter boxes, exact pole, first tail block, and the analytic monotone remainder. Tonelli then gives gamma|A|<=nu(W(A)). No infimum is moved through a radial sum.

Outer recovery is sectional: for each original direction q fix its chosen legal center a(q). Since its compact triangle is contained in open G and triangles depend continuously on direction for fixed center, there is an open V_q such that the same center remains legal and T(q',a(q)) subset G for q' in V_q. Compactness gives a finite subcover; tie-breaking gives a finite Borel selector. Re-run the easy/hard split on this recovered selector and take the infimum over open G.
