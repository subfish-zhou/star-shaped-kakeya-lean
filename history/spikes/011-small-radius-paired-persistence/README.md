# Spike 011 — small-radius near-balanced paired persistence

## Verdict: VALIDATED (paper-level theorem; numeric certificate not yet frozen)

The old fixed-cut two-track lemma lost an **absolute** `4 asin(H/u)`.  This spike
removes that bottleneck.  A cut is chosen only inside the proof, by averaging a
measurable hull of the possibly nonmeasurable direction class.  Directions in a
`beta=asin(H/u)` ball around the cut are discarded; their outer mass is at most
`(2 beta/pi)|A|*`.  On the retained class the two full-chord tracks occupy
opposite oriented half-circles and are exactly additive.  Thus

```text
|Q_u|* >= 2 (R-u)/(R+u) (1-2 asin(H/u)/pi) |A|*.
```

This is selector-independent, proportional for every positive class mass, and
has no frozen projective cut in its statement.

## Why this closes the hybrid bottleneck structurally

For `m<=H, M<=q<1`, the chord is foot-inside and both branches reach `1-q`.
Therefore the theorem pays in the fixed inner annulus `(H,alpha_1)`, below the
frozen CFS support.  It can be added without overlap.  To lift the complementary
part of the old first CFS class, choose `q>1/2` and replace a short slice of
`[alpha_1,alpha_2)` by a one-track action on the complement, whose reach is

```text
R_c = min(sqrt(1/4+H^2), q) > 1/2.
```

No radial atom is double charged.  Since the frozen exact surrogate has a unique
first-class minimum, any sufficiently short nonzero replacement slice raises
both pieces of that class while leaving all other rows untouched.  Reapplying
the refined finite-Choquet assembly proves an exact qualitative strict lift of
the previously certified `q*pi` surrogate witness; it does not optimize the
true-log frozen minimum or the original `C_FS` action class.  The checked
floating witness (`H=alpha_1/2`, `q=0.51`, slice length `0.0034`) reaches the
untouched second-row floor.  It is a scout, not a new advertised decimal.
